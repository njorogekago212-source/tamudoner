The Origin Story

Two food cultures, one spit

In Turkey, chicken plays a major role in the food culture. But noticing that Kenyan chicken naturally tastes even better, our founders were inspired to marry the two — bringing authentic Turkish culinary techniques to Kenya's high-quality local produce to create a spectacular delicacy.

Tamu Döner launched in April 2026, operating under Celebi Foods and Water Equipment Ltd, with a mission to build global harmony through culinary fusion — showcasing how food, water, and organic juices can bridge Turkish and Kenyan communities together for many years to come.
